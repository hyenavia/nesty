Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: November 3, 2026 • Version: 1.1

At Nesty ("we", "our", or "us"), we believe your everyday home management and living habits are strictly your personal business. Nesty is engineered with an offline-first philosophy: your data belongs to you, is stored on your device by default, and is never shared, sold, or used for third-party advertising.

1. Information We Collect

Nesty collects only the minimum information necessary to provide house-tracking features:

  • Local Household Data: Inventory supplies, recurring routines/tasks, prepaid electricity meter readings, water gallon consumption logs, laundry batches, and expense records. This data is stored locally on your device via SQLite / SharedPreferences.
  • Optional Account Information: If you choose to create a cloud backup account, we store your chosen Username, Email address, and authenticated user identifier (UID) managed securely via Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile Data: An optional nickname and profile photo (stored in your device storage, and optionally synchronized to your secure private Firestore document).
  • Device Information & Crash Reports: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic info, app version, and OS version when an unexpected error occurs, solely for stability fixes.
Zero Tracking Guarantee: Nesty does not use tracking cookies, does not display advertisements, does not collect location data, and does not sell or share personal data with data brokers.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To track supplies, predict token depletion and gallon run-out dates locally on your device.
  • To schedule local on-device notifications and reminder alerts for chores and expiring items.
  • To allow multi-device sync or data restoration when you explicitly log in to your account.

3. Cloud Infrastructure & Security

When cloud sync is enabled, your data is transmitted over encrypted TLS/SSL connections and stored in Google Cloud Firestore. Our security rules strictly enforce isolation per user ID (UID): no other user or third party can read or modify your documents.

4. User Account & Data Deletion Policy

We respect your right to be forgotten. In compliance with Google Play Developer Policy and Apple App Store Review Guidelines, users can permanently delete their account and all associated data at any time through either of the methods below.

Method A: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can delete your account and wipe all cloud data instantly without waiting:

  1. Open the Nesty app.
  2. Navigate to the Profile (Profil) tab at the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account (Akun) section.
  4. Tap Delete Account (Hapus Akun).
  5. Enter your current password to confirm your identity.
  6. Once confirmed, your account is immediately deleted from Firebase Auth, your cloud Firestore document is permanently destroyed, your username reservation is freed, and local session caches are erased.

Method B: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can request full account deletion via email:

  1. Send an email from your registered account email address to: support@nesty.app (or developer contact: hyenavia@gmail.com).
  2. Use the email subject line: [Data Deletion Request] - Nesty.
  3. State your registered Username or Email address in the email body.
  4. Our team will verify the request and permanently delete your Firebase user account, usernames entry, and all Firestore records within 7 business days. A confirmation email will be sent upon completion.

Data Retention Policy

Upon account deletion (via in-app confirmation or verified email request), all cloud-hosted data including your profile, supplies records, bills history, and token estimates are permanently removed from our databases. We do not retain backup archives of deleted accounts.

5. Children's Privacy

Nesty does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will promptly delete that information.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
