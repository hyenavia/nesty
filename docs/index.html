Why Nesty?

Living on your own is not just about getting by. It is about enjoying every day without worrying about the small stuff.

  • Proactive reminders before utilities run dry
  • Items running low go straight to your shopping list
  • Split recurring bills with roommates (rent, WiFi, water)
  • Works 100% offline, syncs transparently when online
  • Optional dual sign-in (email or username) + guest mode
  • Full bilingual support: English & Bahasa Indonesia
⚡ Electricity Token Calculates burn rate & predicts when kWh runs out.
💧 Drinking Water Gallon Learns consumption pace; alerts before empty.
🧺 Laundry Lifecycle Track from basket to pickup with costs & receipts.
🛒 Daily Supplies Manage essentials with stepper quantity counters.
🧾 Recurring Bills Never miss rent or WiFi; split shares dynamically.
📊 Living Expenses Weekly & monthly financial pulses with CSV export.
