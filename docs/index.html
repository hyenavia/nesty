Core Features

Designed for Ease, Peace of Mind & Simplicity

Every essential aspect of independent living organized into one distraction-free assistant.

⚡

Offline-First & Instant

Zero loading spinners or network lag. Built on lightning-fast local storage that operates reliably anywhere, anytime.

📦

Smart Stock Tracker

Categorize toiletries, kitchen essentials, and pantry items. Running low items are instantly grouped for easy grocery runs.

🧹

Cleaning Routines

Set manageable cadences for laundry, bedding, and floor cleaning so chores never pile up into stressful weekends.

💧

Token & Gallon Estimator

Keep track of your electricity tokens and water gallons with predictive alerts before they run completely empty.

☁️

Flexible Cloud Sync

Enjoy 100% anonymous offline usage, or connect Firebase to securely sync your data across devices in real time.

🌐

Bilingual & Clean

Full support for English and Bahasa Indonesia with zero ads, zero trackers, and an uncluttered Material 3 interface.
